How to play Koi-Koi

Match the flowers. 48 cards, four for each month. Tap a card in your hand, then a glowing card of the same flower on the table: you take both. No match? The card is laid on the table.

Then the stack turns. The top card of the stack is turned and matched the same way.

Make a yaku (a set, right) from what you have taken. Then choose: Shōbu stops the month and banks your points, or Koi-Koi plays on for more: if you stop later your points are ×2 (×3 after a second call). But if the rival makes a yaku and stops first, you get nothing and they score double.

From the Onsen on, a stop worth 7 or more is doubled.

Keys: ←→ or 1–8 pick · Enter play · K Koi-Koi · S Shōbu · P pause · M mute · Esc back.

House rules: the Sake Cup is an animal and a plain. Extra animals and ribbons are counted once, by Tane and Tan. Four of a month in a deal is re-dealt. A month nobody stops is scored by nobody.
